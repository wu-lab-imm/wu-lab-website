  From August 19 to 25, 2026, the 17th K. H. Kuo Summer School of Electron
  Microscopy and the 2026 International Symposium on Cryo-Electron Microscopy
  were held at Huairou Science City in Beijing. The meeting brought together
  researchers in cryo-electron microscopy and structural biology to discuss
  recent advances in cryo-EM methodology, in situ structural biology,
  atomic-resolution studies of molecular mechanisms and drug discovery, as well
  as the integration of artificial intelligence with cryo-EM.


  Jun Zhou, Yuanzhi Yu and Xinrui Meng from the Wu Lab attended the meeting.
  During the poster sessions, the group presented recent progress in
  understanding the structural and functional mechanisms of neurotransmitter
  transporters.


  Poster P-005, “Substrate recognition and allosteric inhibition of human
  betaine/GABA transporter 1,” presented our work on the human betaine/GABA
  transporter 1 (hBGT1). By combining cryo-EM structures with functional
  studies, this work revealed distinct modes of substrate and inhibitor
  recognition and provided structural insights into the transport cycle and
  allosteric inhibition of BGT1.


  Poster P-025, “Transport and inhibition mechanisms of human glycine
  transporter 2,” presented our study of the human glycine transporter 2
  (hGlyT2). Structural characterization of multiple functional and
  inhibitor-bound states, together with functional analyses, provided further
  insights into substrate transport, conformational transitions and the
  molecular mechanisms of GlyT2 inhibition.


  During the poster sessions, group members discussed membrane-protein sample
  preparation, cryo-EM structure determination, transporter conformational
  dynamics, ligand recognition and structure-guided drug discovery with
  researchers from different institutions.


  Participation in the meeting provided an opportunity to learn about recent
  developments in cryo-EM and structural biology and to exchange ideas with
  colleagues in the field. The Wu Lab will continue to investigate the
  structural and functional mechanisms of membrane proteins, structural
  pharmacology and the application of cryo-EM to complex biological systems.

2026年8月19日至25日，第十七届郭可信电子显微学与晶体学暑期学校暨2026冷冻电镜技术国际研讨会在北京怀柔科学城举行。本次会议汇集了国内外冷冻电子显微学与结构生物学领域的专家学者，围绕冷冻电镜技术与方法学前沿、原位结构生物学、原子分辨率结构机制与药物发现，以及人工智能与冷冻电镜技术融合等方向进行了广泛交流。

吴惊香课题组周俊、虞远智和孟欣睿参加了本次会议。会议期间，课题组以墙报形式展示了近期在神经递质转运体结构与功能研究方面的工作。

其中，P-005 “Substrate recognition and allosteric inhibition of human betaine/GABA transporter 1” 展示了人源甜菜碱/GABA转运体1（hBGT1）的底物识别、构象转换及变构抑制机制研究。该工作结合冷冻电镜结构与功能实验，揭示了不同底物和抑制剂的识别模式，并进一步阐释了BGT1转运循环及变构抑制的结构基础。

P-025 “Transport and inhibition mechanisms of human glycine transporter 2” 展示了人源甘氨酸转运体2（hGlyT2）的转运与抑制机制研究。通过解析不同功能状态及抑制剂结合状态下的结构，并结合功能分析，该研究进一步揭示了GlyT2底物转运、构象变化以及不同类型抑制剂发挥作用的分子机制。

墙报交流期间，课题组成员与来自不同研究机构的同行围绕膜蛋白样品制备、冷冻电镜结构解析、转运体构象变化、配体识别以及结构基础上的药物设计等问题进行了交流和讨论。

此次参会不仅为课题组成员提供了集中了解冷冻电镜技术与结构生物学最新进展的机会，也促进了与同行之间的科研交流。课题组将继续围绕膜蛋白结构与功能机制、结构药理学以及冷冻电镜技术在复杂生物体系研究中的应用开展工作。
